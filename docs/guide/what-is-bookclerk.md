# What is Bookclerk?

Bookclerk gathers the audiobooks you have bought from Audible, Libro.fm,
Chirp and GraphicAudio into one catalog on your own computer, keeps copies
you control, and shelves them where your player already looks. You use it
in a web browser, on the computer that runs it or anywhere on your home
network.

## What it does

- **One library for every store.** Connect each store you buy from, and
  Bookclerk lists all your books together, with covers, series and authors.
  See [Stores](stores.md).
- **Copies you keep.** Bookclerk downloads each book as an ordinary audio
  file with its chapters and cover, and saves it to a folder, a network
  drive or cloud storage. See [Where your books go](where-books-go.md).
- **Plays where you listen.** Save books into the folder your
  Audiobookshelf or Plex server reads, named the way it expects, and new
  books simply appear there.
- **Runs on your hardware.** A spare computer, a home server or a NAS is
  enough. A second computer can [join as a branch](branches.md) and share
  the work.
- **Shared with your household.** Invite the people you live with. Everyone
  sees the same library and keeps their own store accounts and wishlist.
  See [Sharing with your household](sharing.md).
- **Private by design.** There is no Bookclerk account to create. Your
  store sign-ins are stored encrypted on your own computer and sent only to
  the store they belong to. Add-ons run sealed off from your files and
  reach only the sites you approve.

## Is it ready?

Not yet. Bookclerk has no stable release, so for now you build the
development version yourself, and things may change between updates.
[Getting started](getting-started.md) walks you through it.

## Try it without installing

While the hosted demo is running, the [Bookclerk website](https://bookclerk.app/)
has a **Try the demo** button. It gives you your own copy of Bookclerk in
the browser for 30 minutes, with a sample library from a made-up member
called Sam. You can sign in to your stores, scan your library and search
it. Not every store can sign in from the demo yet.

The demo cannot download books. It ends when the 30 minutes are up, or
after 10 minutes without activity. Then it signs your stores out and deletes
everything.
