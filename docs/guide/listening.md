# Listen in the web app

Bookclerk plays the audiobooks it keeps, in your browser, and remembers
where you stopped. Start a book on one device and carry on from the same
place on another.

## Play a book

1. Open **Library**.
2. Press **Play** on a book's row, or open the book and press **Play**.

A book shows **Play** once its audio is acquired. If it has several
audio editions, Bookclerk plays the one you listened to last.

A player bar appears along the bottom of the screen and stays there while
you move between screens. It has:

- back 30 seconds, play or pause, and forward 30 seconds;
- a timeline you can drag to any place in the book;
- the playback speed, from 0.75× to 3×, which Bookclerk remembers in this
  browser;
- a sleep timer that pauses the book after 5 to 60 minutes;
- an arrow that opens the larger now-playing view, and a cross that stops
  the book and closes the bar.

Only one book plays at a time. Starting another book stops the first and
keeps its place.

## Continue listening

Books you are part way through appear on the **Continue listening** shelf
at the top of the Library, most recent first, each with a bar showing how
far you are and the time left at your speed. Press one to carry on.

The shelf hides while you search or filter the Library.

## Bookmarks

Open the now-playing view to keep bookmarks:

1. Type a note if you want one, then press **Add**. The bookmark marks
   the place you are at.
2. Press a bookmark to jump back to it.
3. Press the bin beside a bookmark to remove it.

Bookmarks are yours alone. Other people in the library do not see them.

## Finishing a book

Bookclerk marks a book finished when you reach its last 30 seconds (or the
last 1%, for a long book). To finish one early, open the now-playing view
and press **Mark finished**. A finished book leaves the Continue
listening shelf and counts as finished everywhere else in Bookclerk, such
as the **My progress** filter.

To listen again, press **Play**: a finished book starts from the
beginning.

## Media keys and the lock screen

While a book is loaded, your device's own media controls work with it:
the play and pause keys on a keyboard or headset, the media panel of your
operating system or browser, and the lock screen of a phone. They show the
book's title, author and cover, and can pause, resume, skip 30 seconds
and move along the timeline.

> [!NOTE]
> Buttons for the previous and next track skip 30 seconds back and
> forward, because many headsets and cars have only those.

## Where your place is kept

Your place is kept on the server, for you alone. The player reports it
every 15 seconds while playing, when you pause, and when you close the
page, so another device is at most a few seconds behind.

## If a book does not play

- **"This book cannot be played from this server."** The book is not
  acquired, or its files are kept by another computer of the library.
  Acquire it, or listen from the computer that keeps it.
- **"The browser blocked playback. Press play again."** Some browsers
  refuse to start sound unless you have just pressed something. Press
  play once more.
- **"This book could not be played."** Your browser may not support the
  file's format. Bookclerk sends the file as it is stored and does not
  convert it while playing. MP3 files, and M4B files holding AAC audio
  (which is what almost every M4B holds), play in every current browser.
  An M4B is a wrapper, though, and one holding another kind of audio,
  such as Apple Lossless, plays only in browsers that support that kind.
- **There is no Play button.** The operator account is not a person in
  the library and has nothing to play as. Sign in as yourself. The
  desktop window does not have the player yet; open Bookclerk in a
  browser to listen.
